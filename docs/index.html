In-season leaderboard

Positions QB/RB/WR/TE/K/DST, averaged over scored weeks. Accuracy gap: mean absolute difference, in fantasy points, between the points implied by a source's rank and the points actually scored. Lower is better. Spearman: rank correlation between predicted and actual positional order; 1.0 is a perfect ordering.

The two metrics weight errors differently: Spearman treats every rank inversion equally, while the accuracy gap scales with the points at stake, so a miss at RB2 costs more than one at RB45. A source can lead one metric and trail the other.
